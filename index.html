Returns are compound (geometric) nominal returns, net of fees. Volatility is the annual standard deviation of returns; private assets use de-smoothed figures.

Inflation and objective

Inflation is mean-reverting: persistence is the share of this year's gap from the long-run mean that carries into next year. Inflation uses the same distribution as the assets.

Model settings

Return distribution

Lognormal: normally distributed log returns. Fat tails: a multivariate Student-t with the same volatility, where extreme years are more common and hit all assets together. Keeping the seed fixed makes comparisons between allocations use the same random scenarios.

Summary by horizon

Annualised returns are compound returns over the whole horizon. "Beats objective" means real wealth at the end of the horizon is above what CPI + the spread would have delivered. Max drawdown is the worst peak-to-trough fall within the horizon, using month-end values; time to recover is months from that trough back to the previous peak.
